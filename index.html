Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between FDI (11–48) and Universal (1–32) tooth notation?

Yes. In your Clinic Settings, you can set the default notation system to FDI Two-Digit (widely used in the UK, Middle East, Australia, and Asia), Universal Numbering System (standard in North America), or Palmer notation. Individual practitioners can also toggle their preferred view at any time.

How does the WhatsApp appointment reminder integration work?

Clinsyst connects to the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. When an appointment is scheduled or confirmed, automated interactive messages with 1-click confirmation or rescheduling buttons are delivered directly to the patient's phone. When they reply, their status in your Clinsyst appointment calendar updates automatically.

Can we import our existing patient list and records from Excel?

Yes. You can import patients, historical balances, and inventory spreadsheets via our 1-click CSV importer. On the Pro and Network plans, our customer engineering team provides free, white-glove migration assistance to help transfer your data with zero downtime.

Do we need to install servers or purchase specialized hardware?

No. Clinsyst runs 100% in the cloud. It works smoothly across modern web browsers on Windows PCs, Macs, iPads, and Android tablets. There are no software updates to run or local database backups to manage.

What happens after the 14-day free trial?

You can test all Pro features for 14 days without entering a credit card. At the end of the trial, you can choose the plan that best fits your clinic. If you decide not to continue, your clinical records remain exportable.
