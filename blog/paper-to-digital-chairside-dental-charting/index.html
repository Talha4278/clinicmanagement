Transitioning from Paper Dental Charts to Chairside Tablet Charting (Without Slowing Down Your Operatory)

Direct Answer: Moving a dental clinic from paper folders to chairside tablet charting succeeds when you adopt a phasing strategy: digitize all new and active recall patients on day one rather than back-scanning years of dormant paper archives, and use a touch-first digital dental charting odontogram that records a 5-surface finding in 3 taps or fewer.

1. The "Active Recall First" Digitization Protocol

Answer-First Summary: Do not halt clinic operations to manually transcribe thousands of historical paper files. Instead, import your patient demographics via CSV, and let assistants chart baseline existing restorations (`Filling`, `Crown`, `Missing`, `RCT`) during the first 90 seconds of each patient's next booked visit.

Using Clinsyst's interactive adult (32-tooth) and pediatric (20-tooth) odontograms, a chairside assistant can tap any tooth on an iPad or Android tablet, select existing restorations, and save the baseline arch before the dentist even sits down.

2. Cross-Infection Control & Four-Handed Tablet Charting

Answer-First Summary: Paper charts near the dental unit are a documented cross-contamination vector for aerosols and gloved hands. Medical-grade waterproof tablet sleeves paired with four-handed assistant entry eliminate contaminated paper handling between the operatory and front desk.
