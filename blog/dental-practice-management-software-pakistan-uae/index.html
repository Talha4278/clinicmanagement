1. Why Regional Dental Clinics Are Moving to the Cloud

Whether you run an established teaching clinic like Dentivista Clinic or a multi-chair orthodontic and implant center, relying on paper registers or single-PC offline installers creates immediate bottlenecks:

  • WhatsApp-First Patient Culture: In both Pakistan and the GCC, patients coordinate appointments, post-op instructions, and payment slips almost exclusively over WhatsApp rather than email or SMS.
  • FDI (`11–48`) & Universal (`1–32`) Flexibility: BDS and FCPS clinicians trained in Pakistan, the UK, and the Middle East chart using ISO 3950 FDI numbering, while visiting North American specialists often prefer Universal numbering. Clinsyst lets clinics toggle between both in 1 click.
  • Localized Pricing & Currency Formatting: Clinsyst supports native PKR (Pakistani Rupee), AED (UAE Dirham), SAR (Saudi Riyal), and USD billing, starting at just PKR 5,999/month for Solo Practices in Pakistan or $39/month internationally.
