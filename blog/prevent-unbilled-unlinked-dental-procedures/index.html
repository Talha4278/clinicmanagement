Troubleshooting Missing Clinical Notes & Unlinked Procedures in Dental Practices

Direct Answer: Unbilled dental procedures happen when a clinician performs an add-on restoration, periapical radiograph, or emergency pulpotomy chairside without linking the finding on the digital dental charting odontogram to the front-desk treatment and invoice queue. Enforcing a closed-loop `Odontogram Finding → Scheduled Treatment → Invoice Line Item` pipeline eliminates this revenue leakage.

1. Why 11–14% of Chairside Add-On Procedures Go Unbilled

Answer-First Summary: During busy clinic hours, a patient booked for scaling and polishing often asks the dentist to "also fix a chipped filling on tooth 16." If the doctor completes the composite restoration but verbally tells the patient to pay at reception without logging the tooth action, the front desk frequently bills only the originally booked hygiene visit.

In Clinsyst, clicking any tooth on the color-coded odontogram triggers the "Schedule Treatment for Tooth" action, which immediately logs the tooth number, doctor ID, and procedure cost in the shared clinic database.

2. End-of-Day Clinical-to-Financial Reconciliation Checklist

Answer-First Summary: Every evening before closing the clinic register, compare three numbers in Clinsyst: (1) Appointments marked `Completed`, (2) Treatments marked `Completed`, and (3) Invoices generated for the day.
  • Step 1: Verify every completed appointment has a saved SOAP examination note or treatment entry.
  • Step 2: Check multi-stage procedures (RCTs and dental implants) to confirm partial installment invoices match the clinical stage completed today.
  • Step 3: Review the digital patient dossier for any pending balance flags before booking the patient's next recall slot.
